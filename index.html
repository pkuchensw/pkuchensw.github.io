Siwei Chen

Undergraduate, Yuanpei College, Peking University

I am an undergraduate at Yuanpei College, Peking University, majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology. I work with Prof. Bin Cui and Prof. Xupeng Miao at PKU, and Prof. Fan Lai at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research focuses on efficient LLM algorithms and systems, especially reinforcement learning, post-training, and diffusion language models. I am also interested in recursive self-improvement and context management for long-horizon agents. I study how algorithm and system design can work together to improve training and inference efficiency without sacrificing model capability.
